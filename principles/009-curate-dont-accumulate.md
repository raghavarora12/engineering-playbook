# Curate, don't accumulate

**Every component, database, region, and nine is permanent operational surface — add them only when
they earn it.**

Systems grow by accretion: things get added, nothing gets removed, because no one is assigned to
remove them. Restraint — the discipline to say no and to delete — is the scarce senior skill.

*Ignore it and:* you end up operating six datastores added for the convenience of six teams, and
carried by everyone who holds the pager.
