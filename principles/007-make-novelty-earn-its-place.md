# Make novelty earn its place

**Default to boring, proven technology; new tech must clear a higher bar than "interesting."**

Every novel component brings immature tooling, thin in-house expertise, and unknown failure modes.
Innovation tokens are finite — spend them on what actually differentiates the product, not the
plumbing.

*Ignore it and:* you're the one debugging a fashionable stack alone at 3am, with no prior art and no
Stack Overflow answer waiting.
