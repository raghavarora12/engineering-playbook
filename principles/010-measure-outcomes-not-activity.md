# Measure outcomes, not activity

**Measure system health and delivery outcomes; never rank people by lines, commits, or story points.**

Activity metrics are vanity — trivial to game and weakly tied to value. When a measure becomes a
target it stops measuring anything (Goodhart), and activity metrics become targets the instant they
appear in a review.

*Ignore it and:* you measure output and you'll get output — more code, more commits, more points —
and not one unit more of value. See [[engineering-metrics]].
