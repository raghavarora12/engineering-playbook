# Every decision names its cost

**No architectural decision is complete until it states what it costs and when it would be the wrong
call.**

An option presented with no downside is an option not yet understood. The tradeoff *is* the
decision; the justification is just the half that's comfortable to write.

*Ignore it and:* decisions sold as free get quietly reversed the moment their hidden cost surfaces —
almost always in production, almost always under load.
